Support

Use this page for help with the Peptide Library iOS app: how it works, your on-device journal, accessibility, privacy, or a problem you want to report.

How to contact us

Open a GitHub Issue with your question or a description of the problem. Please do not include journal notes, check-ins, or other personal information.

github.com/jaypark1990/support-library-site/issues

What we can help with

  • App questions and how to find research pages
  • App functionality, including interests, Explore, and My Journey
  • Journal and check-in questions (the journal stays on your iPhone)
  • Accessibility, including Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Face ID lock
  • Privacy questions
  • Reporting a problem or incorrect research presentation

For questions about medical conditions, medications, treatment, or personal health decisions, please consult a qualified healthcare professional. Peptide Library does not provide medical advice.
