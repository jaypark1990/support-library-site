Little Us Support

Hi, we're here to help

Little Us is a small, private place for friends and couples to look after each other. If something isn't working, or someone made you feel unsafe, tell us and a real person will read it.

Contact: jaypark.normal@gmail.com (put "Little Us" in the subject) Typical reply: within 2 business days. Safety reports about content in the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

Please include: your app version (iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Little Us), your iPhone model and a short description of what happened.

Please don't include: invitation links, sign-in codes, private photos, or other people's personal details. To report content, use the report button in the app (see below). That way the reviewer sees only the item you reported.

In danger right now? Contact your local emergency services. Little Us can't respond to emergencies.

Frequently asked questions

How do I invite someone?

Open Space, tap People and settings, and share the invitation card. You can also tap Make room for your person if you're on your own. Only the person who made the space can invite. A space holds up to four people, including you.

Each invitation:

  • works once,
  • expires after 24 hours,
  • stops working if you make a new one or cancel it.

You can also leave a first care with the invitation, and it arrives when they join.

My invitation won't open

Ask the person who invited you for a fresh one. The link may have expired, already been used, or been replaced by a newer invitation. A space that already has four people can't take anyone else. If either of you has blocked the other, the invitation won't open.

How do I delete my account?

Go to You → gear icon (Account settings) → Delete account. Confirm with the same Apple account you use to sign in. We delete your profile, progress and Stars, and the cares, notes, promises and rituals you shared. Spaces you share with others carry on without you. We also ask Apple to remove Little Us from your Sign in with Apple list. If Apple can't confirm that, the app shows you how to do it yourself in Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple. This can't be undone.

If you subscribe to Space Plus, cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions before deleting your account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

How do I report someone or something?

  • A care: tap Report care on the care, then choose a reason.
  • A note in Keeps: open the note's ⋯ menu, then choose Report note.
  • A promise: tap Report promise.
  • A person: in Space → People and settings, tap ⋯ next to their name, then choose Report.
  • A space name: in Space → People and settings, choose Report this room's name.

Reports go to a private review queue. A reviewer sees the item you reported and the reason you chose. They act within 24 hours. The person you reported isn't told who reported them.

How do I block someone?

Go to Space → People and settings, tap ⋯ next to their name, then choose Block person. You'll leave every space you share with them, and neither of you can join a space the other is in. Your XP and Stars stay with you. To undo it, go to You → Account settings → Blocked people. To share a space again, you'll need a new invitation.

What are Stars?

Stars are little thank-yous for showing up. You earn 2 Stars when you check off a care someone sent you, for up to three check-offs a day. The daily parcel can add a few more. Spend them on room themes and companion accessories. Stars have no cash value. You can't buy, sell or transfer them. Promises are separate: they're between you and your people and never cost Stars.

Why isn't a care a proof?

Checking off a care means the person tapped Done. Little Us can't see what happens in real life, so a check-off is a kind signal, not evidence. Please don't use Little Us for anything where proof matters, such as medication, health or safety.

I'm not getting care alerts

Open You → Account settings → Care alerts and make sure alerts are on. Then check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Little Us. Alerts are linked to your sign-in, so signing out turns them off on that iPhone.

What's the sample room?

It's a practice room that lives only on your iPhone. Nothing in it is sent to anyone. Your real spaces show the names of the people you've invited.

I got a new iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple account, and your spaces and progress come back. Cares still waiting to send on the old iPhone stay there until that iPhone is online again.

How do I cancel or manage Space Plus?

Space Plus is billed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Little Us to change plans or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Space Plus doesn't show up after you subscribe, open You → Space Plus → Restore purchases.
