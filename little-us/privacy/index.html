Little Us Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Little Us is a small, private place to look after the people you care about. You share a space with up to four people, including yourself, and send each other small cares, notes and promises. This policy explains what we keep, why we keep it, who can see it, and how to remove it.

The short version

  • We keep only what Little Us needs to work: your account, what you share in your spaces, and the progress you earn.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell or share your information for advertising.
  • The people in your space see what you send them. Nobody else in Little Us can find you or browse your spaces.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. That removes your account and what you shared.

Who we are

Little Us is offered by Jay Park ("we", "us"), the seller shown on the App Store. You can reach us at jaypark.normal@gmail.com.

Photos you choose to share

You can add one photo to a Keeps note, a care or a personal promise. Little Us first makes a smaller copy on your iPhone: at most 1,200 pixels across, re-encoded without the original's location data. Only that copy is uploaded, to private storage that no app can read directly. Little Us only sees the photos you pick in Apple's photo picker, never your whole library.

  • Keeps note photo: you and the people you chose, once they've opened the note, for 30 days.
  • Care photo: you and the person you sent the care to, for 30 days.
  • Promise photo: you right away; the person you made the promise for only after they unwrap it. It's kept for 30 days after the unwrap, or 90 days after you sent it if it's never unwrapped. A declined promise loses its photo.

After that, the stored image is deleted automatically. The words of the note, care or promise can stay. Photos count toward a shared limit of five a day.

Notifications (only if you turn on alerts)

If you turn on care alerts, Little Us stores your iPhone's push token (an address Apple gives us for sending notifications to this iPhone) and links it to your signed-in session. We also store the app's build number, so an alert is worded in a way your version of the app can show.

Alerts go through Apple Push Notification service. A new-care alert says only "You have a new little care." Reaction, "thinking of you" and response alerts include the other person's display name, for example "Sam is thinking of you" or "Sam finished your care 💛". No alert includes a care's title, a promise's title or a note's words.

Space Plus purchases (only if you subscribe)

Space Plus is an optional subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your card or bank details. To give you and your rooms Space Plus, we and our subscription provider RevenueCat receive:

  • your Little Us account ID (used as the purchase account, so your subscription follows you, not your iPhone);
  • which plan you bought, its purchase, trial, renewal and expiry dates, its transaction identifiers, and the App Store country and currency;
  • whether the subscription is active, in a trial, in a billing grace period, cancelled or refunded.

We use this to turn Space Plus on and off for you and the rooms it covers. RevenueCat also uses it to give us subscription reports, such as how many trials started or renewed. Those reports never include anything you share in Little Us. People in your rooms can see that a room has Space Plus; they aren't told who pays.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your contacts, location, health data, payment card details, advertising identifiers, browsing history or crash analytics. We don't use your data to profile you or target ads.

What stays on your iPhone

Little Us keeps some data only on your iPhone, so it works offline and opens quickly:

  • Your sign-in session, in the iPhone Keychain, available on this device only.
  • A cached copy of your spaces, cares waiting to send, and drafts, in the app's private storage.
  • A small summary for the Home Screen widget. It doesn't show the space name unless you turn that on.
  • Settings such as sounds and the widget style.
  • Sample room. The sample room you can try before inviting anyone exists only on this iPhone. Nothing in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Signing out or deleting your account clears this data. Deleting the app removes it too. iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted until you sign in again or reset the iPhone.

Where your data is stored

Your account and everything you share are stored with Supabase, our database and hosting provider, in its United States West (North California, us-west-1) region. Data travels between the app and Supabase over encrypted HTTPS connections. Little Us isn't end-to-end encrypted: the service can technically read stored content. We look at content only when it has been reported to us, when we need to fix a problem you've asked us about, or when the law requires it.

If you live outside the United States, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States.

Deleting your account

Go to You → Account settings (gear icon) → Delete account and confirm with the same Apple account you use to sign in.

When you delete your account:

  • We delete your profile, your memberships, the cares you sent and received, your Keeps notes, promises, rituals, reactions, progress, Stars, cosmetics, push registrations, blocks and the reports you made. Your sign-in sessions end.
  • Spaces you share with others carry on without you. If you owned one, the person who joined first becomes its owner.
  • A group promise you started keeps its shared progress for the others. Its title is replaced with "A shared activity", and your name is removed from it.
  • Any photo you shared is deleted from storage by the next hourly cleanup.
  • We ask Apple to revoke Little Us's access to your Apple account. If Apple can't confirm that, the app shows you how to remove Little Us yourself in Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

Account deletion and an App Store subscription are separate. Deleting your account doesn't cancel Space Plus. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions first; the app reminds you if Space Plus is active.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: your profile is in the app. Contact us for a copy of other data we hold about you.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app, or contact us if you can't.
  • Notifications: turn alerts off in Account settings or in iPhone Settings.
  • Photos: share a photo only if you want to. The app never reads your photo library on its own.
  • Leave or block: leave a space or block someone at any time from Space → People and settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to how we use your data or complaining to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us at jaypark.normal@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Little Us isn't made for children. You must be at least 13, or older if the law where you live requires it. If you're under 18, use Little Us only with a parent or guardian's permission. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we'll delete that account. If you think a child under 13 is using Little Us, contact us.

Tracking and advertising

Little Us doesn't track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics of how you use the app. RevenueCat's subscription reports cover purchases only. We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before the change applies.
